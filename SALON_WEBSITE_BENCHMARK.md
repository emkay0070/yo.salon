## Common Design Patterns & Features

### Color Schemes
- **Bold accent colors:** Orange (#FF622B), Blue (#2779a7)
- **Neutral bases:** Black (#000000), White (#ffffff), Gray (#9C9C9C)
- **High contrast** for readability and visual impact

### Typography & Layout
- Clean, modern sans-serif fonts
- Strong hierarchy with large headings
- Generous white space
- Mobile-responsive layouts

### Essential Sections
1. **Hero Section** - Fullscreen with strong imagery
2. **About/Story** - Brand narrative and craftsmanship focus
3. **Services/Menu** - Clear service offerings
4. **Team/Barbers** - Staff profiles and gallery
5. **Locations** - Multiple locations with details
6. **FAQ** - Common questions answered
7. **Contact** - Easy booking and contact options
8. **Blog** - Content marketing and trends

### Interactive Elements
- Microinteractions for engagement
- Smooth animations and transitions
- Video content (especially in About sections)
- Unusual navigation patterns for memorability

### Technical Features
- Responsive design (mobile-first approach)
- E-commerce integration (booking, products)
- Big background images for visual impact
- Flat design aesthetic
- Modern frameworks (Framer, WordPress/Elementor)

## Recommendations for Yo Salon

### Design Direction
1. **Color Palette:** Consider a bold accent color (orange or blue) with neutral black/white base
2. **Visual Style:** Flat design with big background images and clean typography
3. **Layout:** Mobile-first responsive design with fullscreen hero section

### Must-Have Features
1. **Hero Section:** Fullscreen with high-quality imagery
2. **About Page:** Include video content showcasing craftsmanship
3. **Team Gallery:** Professional photos of stylists/barbers
4. **Service Menu:** Clear pricing and service descriptions
5. **Booking System:** Online appointment scheduling
6. **Locations:** Multiple location details if applicable
7. **FAQ Section:** Address common customer questions
8. **Blog:** Hair care tips and trend content
9. **Contact Form:** Easy inquiry submission
10. **Social Proof:** Testimonials and reviews

### Technical Stack Recommendations
- **Framework:** Next.js (already in use) or Framer for modern animations
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS for responsive design
- **Booking:** Integrate booking system (Calendly, Square, or custom)
- **CMS:** Consider headless CMS for blog and content management

### Unique Differentiators
- Focus on craftsmanship and artistry (like Hagi's)
- Emphasize luxury/exclusive positioning (like Don Barber)
- Showcase authentic, local character (like Porem)
- Modern, trend-focused approach (like FreshCut)
